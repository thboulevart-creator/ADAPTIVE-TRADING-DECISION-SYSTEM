## 3. Adopted Gate-A architecture

The statistical methodology funnel is organized as:

```text
LAYER 0 — UPSTREAM EXPERIMENT ADMISSIBILITY
LAYER 1 — CORE STATISTICAL REQUIREMENTS
LAYER 2 — CONDITIONAL / DOWNSTREAM REQUIREMENTS
LAYER 3 — PROVENANCE / RECONSTRUCTIBILITY
```

SMF inherits upstream data, temporal, execution, provenance and reproducibility validity. It does not create a competing replacement governance layer.

A statistical interpretation is blocked when an applicable upstream prerequisite is not qualified for the relevant surface.

## 5. Material failure-mode coverage added by V0.3

The adopted Gate-A design explicitly preserves coverage for:

- upstream experiment admissibility;
- estimand drift and horizon / aggregation switching;
- sample selection and non-neutral attrition;
- optional stopping / repeated peeking;
- post-selection inference;
- search-universe uncertainty and non-invented trial counts;
- evidence aggregation semantics and no metric voting;
- benchmark / null / direction validity;
- generalized dependence, overlap, clustering and pseudo-replication;
- failure-to-reject versus evidence of absence;
- tail-support scarcity and unseen-tail limits of empirical resampling;
- conditional-sample adequacy;
- evidence concentration / influential observations;
- execution-cost uncertainty relative to claimed economic effect;
- return aggregation / compounding semantics;
- parameter uncertainty distinct from Monte-Carlo numerical precision;
- post-hoc regime segmentation remaining exploratory unless independently confirmed.
