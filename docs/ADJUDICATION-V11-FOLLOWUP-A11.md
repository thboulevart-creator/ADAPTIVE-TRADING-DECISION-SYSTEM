# ADJUDICATION V11 FOLLOW-UP — A-11

## Statut

DECISION EXPLICITE — V11-01 / V11-04 / V11-07 adjudicated for minimal V12 correction.

## Scope

The V12 correction is limited to:

- V11-01 — exact observation universe/cardinality test;
- V11-04 — explicit blocking effect of unresolved normative questions;
- V11-07 — explicit BAR_IN_PROGRESS to BAR_CLOSED observation-universe continuity.

No decision is introduced for:

- late ticks;
- contradiction/cycle handling;
- V11-02 future contamination beyond the adopted causal test;
- V11-03 snapshot identity as a new normative identity mechanism;
- V11-05 temporal-relation domain beyond A-11;
- V11-06 event_time versus availability_time;
- V11-08 naming.

## Corpus basis

A-11 explicitly makes `ordered_ticks` the unique normative field and requires complete representation of established order relations, without inventing unestablished relations. The absence of a relation is not itself proof of incomparability. The serialization must be deterministic according to stable observation identity.

## Decisions

### V11-01 — ACCEPTED

A set comparison is insufficient to establish exact correspondence when multiplicity or identity collision could be masked. V12 therefore requires individual observation correspondence through count and multiset identity equality.

### V11-04 — ACCEPTED

A question explicitly remaining unresolved cannot be converted into an implicit normative implementation choice. Where such a question is necessary for conformance, canonical representation, or required reproducibility, it blocks PASS and prevents CLOSED/FROZEN until adjudicated.

### V11-07 — ACCEPTED

The final `BAR_CLOSED` representation concerns the same temporal bar and must contain exactly the primary observations assigned to that bar under the normative partition. Closure may expose newly established relations but cannot rewrite historical in-progress snapshots.

## Result

V12 is authorized as a minimal candidate correction. It must undergo a fresh adversarial conformance audit before any closure or freeze decision.
